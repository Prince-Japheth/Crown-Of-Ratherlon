# Simon’s early grey: cousinly teasing

Added a brief exchange in chapter 4 after Gareth’s arrival, where Elyra is already laughing and Simon has welcomed both cousins. This avoids joking over the murdered watchman in their first meeting.

Elyra teases Simon about greying at twenty-eight. He blames her arrival; she threatens to leave him Gareth; Simon chooses to keep both cousins and the grey. His reply shows affection and wit without explaining away every sign of wear or changing his established age.

No new lore, names or chronology are introduced.
