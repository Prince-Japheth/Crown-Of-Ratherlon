## Clarification: relief means death

The user clarified that rider-loss grief persists until the Nyxen dies; wishes for relief mean a peaceful death, not recovery or happiness in solitude. Oak’s continued survival is distressing endurance of accumulated grief.

- Chapter 3: `Oak, still alive? She hoped he would not have to linger much longer.`
- Chapter 14: `I did not think he would last the winter. Poor old beast. May Vael let him die in peace.`

These replace the earlier implications that survival is welcome or grief can pass while Oak remains alive. Surprise and sympathy remain brief; no strategic speculation is added.
