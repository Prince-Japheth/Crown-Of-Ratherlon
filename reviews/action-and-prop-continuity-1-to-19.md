# Action and prop continuity pass, chapters 1–19

This focused pass follows clothing, hand use, carried objects, weapon handling and character positions. All nineteen chapters were searched for candidate transitions; suspicious sequences were checked in their surrounding scenes. It is not a claim that every sentence or every possible continuity problem has been exhausted.

## Exact corrections

### 1. chapters/2.md

Bare hands must become gloved hands before the gloves can be adjusted.

Replace:

```text
Then he stepped back and drew his gloves straight, one finger at a time, and the Handler at the gate studied the sky with great attention.
```

With:

```text
Then he stepped back, pulled on his gloves, and drew each finger snug. The handler at the gate studied the sky with great attention.
```
