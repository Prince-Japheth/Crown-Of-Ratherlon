# Review after chapter 18 and chapter 19 delivery

Scope: the supplied map, chapters 1–18, and the `.agents` rule corpus. This is a continuity and structural review with targeted prose corrections, not a rewrite or a claim that every stylistic blemish is gone. The newest explicit user canon controls conflicts; the measured map and fixed age table control route and date disputes. Attached review instructions are source material, not new author commands.

## Firm decisions

- “Men of Timber and Caliber” belongs to Deepwood timber crews who cut, measure, raft and fit the crown’s timber. It is a working boast about honest measure and dependable workmanship, not a gun reference or a new house motto. Deepwood’s position southwest of Foldhall and the timber route make the roost works a natural first use.
- Chapter 19 is WYNN, six days after chapter 18, in the twins’ first spring. Tomas arrives from Greywater by a day’s ride. The twins and Brenna are infants. The unnamed female pup chooses nobody. Lark and Thorn remain in the Eastverge; Hart is home after a timed return flight.
- Capable characters pursue the steps available to them and share relevant facts. Their constraints are jurisdiction, evidence, weather, distance, custody and competing duties. Do not turn every constraint into a kidnapping or an injury.
- Chapter 20 formally names Rowan Master of Coin. The empty Hand remains a governing policy, not an appointment postponed for twenty years. The discrepancy between the office Rowan wanted and the one offered preserves his grievance without making Rodrik forget a direct promise.
