# The Cause of the First Ashen War

The First Ashen War (fought roughly ten years before Book 1, under King Aldren VIII) was not a random border skirmish that escalated, nor was it a blind suicide charge against the massive Nyxen, Oak. It was a calculated, desperate gamble by the Ashen Kingdom that wove together geography, timing, and a failed trap. 

## 1. The Desperation (Containment)
The Rathen expansion had just reached its final eastward limit. Foldhall had begun reinforcing the old Kelward watch-stones (the Redcut among them) and raising new ones right up against the Ashen mountain passes. The Ashen King realized that once these stone fortresses were fully garrisoned and supplied, his kingdom would be permanently choked out and boxed in. He had to break the watch-stones before the mortar dried, or accept slow suffocation. 

## 2. The Miscalculation (Geography & Timing)
The Ashen King waited for the perfect moment. He struck when King Aldren VIII and Oak were bogged down on the far Western Shore (dealing with a Vaelcross trade dispute or a Deepwood rebellion). Because Ratherlon is the longest kingdom on the continent, the Ashen calculated they had a window: they could pour over the borders and permanently smash the Eastverge watch-stones before a king could untangle his army and fly a massive beast across the entire landmass. 

## 3. The Failed Trap (Hubris)
The Ashen knew Aldren VIII would eventually come. They planned for it. For years, they had secretly forged massive, iron-limbed scorpion-ballistas and hid them in the deep gullies of the Eastverge. The invasion was bait to draw Oak into a specific kill-zone where they believed they could finally shoot the great beast out of the sky. 

**The Result:** 
Aldren VIII abandoned the western conflict and flew east faster than the Ashen anticipated. When Oak descended into the Eastverge, the Ashen sprang their trap—but it failed. Oak was too large, too heavily furred, and too devastating. The beast shattered the iron scorpions and broke the Ashen lines with a roar that burst men's eardrums (this is the battle where Ser Corren lost half his ear). 

The trap failed, but the massive Ashen army was already in the Eastverge, and the brutal, grinding First Ashen War had begun. Rodrik (Aldren IX) fought in this war as a young prince, defying his father to ride Hart in the vanguard because he refused to let his men bleed alone.
