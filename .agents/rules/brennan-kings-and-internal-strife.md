# The Brennan kings were not all noble, and the realm was never at peace

**SPOILER FLAG:** Queen Seren and the war with her brother are spoilers for a future book. Never name Seren in Book 1 prose. Use them only to check the timeline.

## 1. You were right: canon was already thin, and it was mostly noble

The `.agents` files already contained some darkness that is easy to forget:

- The Great Civil War before Aldren I.
- Aldren III's child, killed by ritual (the only king you remembered as tainted).
- Seren's war with her brother Osbert.

## 4. The Nyxenless king: Aldren IV, the Grounded King

- Son of Leofric and Seraphine. Grew up in the shadow of a father who flew across the sea and left his mother weeping.
- At nineteen, flying with his first Nyxen **Fennel** over the Rivermark, he fell. Fennel caught him badly and broke a forelimb in doing so. The beast lived and never flew again. Aldren IV broke three ribs and, worse, his nerve. He developed a terror of heights.
- He **refused a second Nyxen**, saying he would not replace a friend. The old beast lived in the Foldhall roosts until it died, and the king visited it every morning.
- He never flew to war in his lifetime. His brother, **Prince Hallan**, flew in his stead and was the realm's real sword. The crown relied on ground heavy cavalry and loaned knights, above all the **Winter Knights** of the South, whose prestige grew in these years.
- The result: the Three Dukes' Quarrel, which the crown could only mediate. Lords took note that a king without wings could be defied.
- He was a kind man and a poor king. Both facts are true. His reign is why the realm still remembers that a Brennan without a beast is half a king.

## 5. Why knights are still needed in the interior

Use these when a reader might ask "why would anyone still have knights?"

1. A king cannot fly to every skirmish. Only the largest matters get a royal rider.
2. There are only about a dozen riders in the family at any time. The rest of the fighting is done on the ground.
3. Every region has had fighting in living memory: timber thieves, river lords, Vaelcross raiders, harbor feuds, highway brotherhoods, the cold roads, the Eastverge raid seasons.
4. Succession quarrels come roughly every two generations, and each leaves lords wary of being caught without swords.
5. The crown has no large standing army outside Crownfold. Everyone else depends on knights.
6. The Winter Knights have a duty (the cold roads) that does not depend on peace, so they never decay.
7. A tourney is the one place the realm meets itself, and it needs knights.
