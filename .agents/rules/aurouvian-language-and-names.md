# The Aurouvian Language and Names

Aurouvian is the mother tongue of Queen Saeraphyn, brought to Foldhall when she married Aldren III.. The royal family still speaks it as a private language for intimacy, whispered asides, and muttered profanity, though two hundred years apart has caused it to drift from the original tongue. 

Linguistically, Aurovian names are characterised by recurring Aurovian naming patterns, including distinctive vowel combinations, recurring syllabic structures, and consonant clusters. Their sound is often melodic, archaic, and occasionally sharp.

## 1. The Roots
* **Rhae / Rae:** Fire, hearth, or a burning star.
* **Dae:** Deep water, the tide, or the moon (which pulls the tide).
* **Ae / Aen / Ah:** The sky, the heavens, or open air.
* **Fay / Faya:** Wind, breath, or the gale.
* **El / Ely:** Silver, twilight, or the shifting of light.
* **Cae:** Stone, foundation, or bedrock.
* **Qaen:** Storm, lightning, or sudden violence.
* **Hein:** Memory, truth, or the past.
* **Beh:** Heart, blood, or the living pulse.
* **Loh:** Gold, dawn, or the rising sun.
* **Doh:** Shadow, night, or the unseen.
* **Joh:** Spirit, winter, or frost.
* **Bae / Bael:** Steel, blade, or valor.
* **Xen:** Secret, shadow, or the hidden.
* **Xaen:** Eclipse, the abyss, or absolute dark.
* **Sae:** Celestial, star, or holy.
* **Vic:** Conquest, victory, or the bleeding crown.
* **Xyr / Xyra:** Scythe, execution, or the severing of ties.
* **Xar:** Slaughter, bloodlust, or ruin (often mid-root).

## 2. The Suffixes
* **-ya / -naya:** "Daughter of" or "Bringer of" (feminine).
* **-ra / -aera / -aerah / -irah / -ira / -eirah / -yra:** "Song," "Voice," "Grace," or "Light."
* **-on / -an / -ion / -ian / -or / -nor:** "Son of," "Reader," or "Shield" (masculine).
* **-ien / -eyen / -heyen:** "Wanderer," "Breath," or "Spirit" (often feminine or gender-neutral).
* **-ella / -nella:** "Little" or "Beloved."
* **-sehn:** "Iron" or "Truth" (often used as a mid-root).
* **-yoh:** "Glory," "Sun," or "Vow" (often used as a mid-root).
* **-heara:** "Crown" or "Queen."
* **-es / -ys / -aerys:** "Warden," "Keeper," "Born of," or "Edge."
* **-myean:** "Dreamer," "Prince," or "Shadow" (masculine).
* **-phyn / -fyn:** "Wing," "Soul," or "Flame."

## 3. Name Etymologies

**Aeian** *(Ae + ian)*
*Meaning:* "Reader of the Heavens."
*Lore:* A softer variation of Aeion. Where Aeion is the sky's shield, Aeian is the scholar who reads it.

**Aeien** *(Ae + ien)*
*Meaning:* "The Sky's Breath," "Wanderer of the Heavens," or "Falling Star."
*Lore:* The third variation of the Ae- masculine root. Its exact meaning shifts depending on context—it can mean a traveler who doesn't stay in one place, or a sharper, more martial "arrow from the sky."

**Aeion** *(Ae + ion)*
*Meaning:* "Son of the Sky" or "Shield of the Heavens."
*Lore:* A high, noble masculine name. In old Aurouvian myth, it might have been the name of a legendary figure who first charted the stars.

**Aenmyean** *(Aen + myean)*
*Meaning:* "Prince of the Sky" or "Dreamer of the Heavens."
*Lore:* A lofty, poetic masculine name. Often given to scholars, astronomers, or dreamers.

**Aensehnion** *(Aen + sehn + ion)*
*Meaning:* "True Shield of the Heavens" or "The Iron Son of the Sky."
*Lore:* A fierce, regal masculine name, given to princes expected to be unyielding protectors of the realm.

**Aesehlla** *(Ae + sehn + ella)*
*Meaning:* "Beloved True Sky" or "Little Iron Heaven."
*Lore:* An affectionate feminine name, taking the heavy *sehn* (iron) and softening it with *ella*. It implies someone precious but resilient.

**Aheirah** *(Ah + eirah)*
*Meaning:* "Grace of the Heavens" or "Sky's Light."
*Lore:* A high, celestial feminine name. It uses the softer *Ah* variation of the sky root, implying breath, height, and blinding light.

**Baeheyen** *(Bae + heyen)*
*Meaning:* "Spirit of Steel" or "Wanderer of Valor."
*Lore:* A fierce, untamed masculine or gender-neutral name. Given to those expected to forge their own path by the strength of their sword.

**Behaera** *(Beh + aera)*
*Meaning:* "The Heart's Song" or "Grace of the Blood."
*Lore:* A warm, fiercely loyal feminine name. It suggests someone who speaks with absolute passion and leads with their emotions.

**Behaerah** *(Beh + aerah)*
*Meaning:* "Light of the Blood" or "Grace of the Heart."
*Lore:* A passionate, radiant feminine name. It suggests someone whose presence brings life and fierce loyalty to a room.

**Behaerys** *(Beh + aerys)*
*Meaning:* "Born of the Heart" or "Warden of the Blood."
*Lore:* A strong, noble masculine name. Historically given to sons who were expected to hold the family together or guard the royal line.

**Caeien** *(Cae + ien)*
*Meaning:* "Spirit of the Stone" or "Wanderer of the Bedrock."
*Lore:* A gender-neutral name. It implies someone who is deeply grounded yet refuses to be tied down to one place.

**Caenor** *(Cae + nor)*
*Meaning:* "The Stone Shield" or "Foundation."
*Lore:* A rugged, blunt masculine name. It implies someone who is unmovable, dependable, and deeply grounded.

**Caesehnira** *(Cae + sehn + ira)*
*Meaning:* "True Light of the Foundation" or "The Iron Grace of the Stone."
*Lore:* A sturdy yet elegant feminine name. It implies someone who is unbreakable but still moves with beauty.

**Caesehnya** *(Cae + sehn + ya)*
*Meaning:* "The Iron Daughter of the Stone."
*Lore:* A traditional, deeply grounded feminine name. It implies a woman who is the absolute bedrock of her family.

**Caesehnys** *(Cae + sehn + ys)*
*Meaning:* "Edge of the True Stone" or "Warden of the Iron Foundation."
*Lore:* A heavy, unyielding masculine name. It implies a defender who stands as a bulwark against any storm.

**Caeyohra** *(Cae + yoh + ra)*
*Meaning:* "Song of Stone's Glory" or "Sunlight on the Foundation."
*Lore:* A bright, majestic feminine name. Suggests an unbreakable spirit that brings light and hope to a stronghold.

**Daeheara** *(Dae + heara)*
*Meaning:* "Queen of the Deep Water" or "Crown of the Tide."
*Lore:* A massive, imposing name. It suggests immense, quiet power—the kind of power that pulls ships under without making a sound.

**Daemyean** *(Dae + myean)*
*Meaning:* "Prince of the Deep Tide" or "Dreamer of the Moon."
*Lore:* A melodic, mysterious masculine name. It implies someone who is quiet, contemplative, and deeply connected to the unseen currents of the world.

**Daenaya** *(Dae + naya)*
*Meaning:* "Daughter of the Deep Tide" or "Bringer of the Moon."
*Lore:* A quieter, heavier name than Rhaenaya. It was often given to girls born during sea-storms or high tides.

**Daesehnys** *(Dae + sehn + ys)*
*Meaning:* "The Iron Edge of the Deep Tide."
*Lore:* A dangerous, sharp masculine name. It implies someone with the crushing, inescapable force of the ocean.

**Dohirah** *(Doh + irah)*
*Meaning:* "Song of the Night" or "Shadow's Grace."
*Lore:* A quiet, elusive feminine name. It suggests someone who operates unseen or brings calm to the dark.

**Elayien** *(Elay + ien)*
*Meaning:* "Wanderer of the Twilight" or "Breath of Silver."
*Lore:* A beautiful, flowing feminine name. It implies someone who moves quietly through the world, bringing a soft, shifting light to dark places.

**Elsehnor** *(El + sehn + or)*
*Meaning:* "True Silver Shield" or "The Iron Shield of Twilight."
*Lore:* A highly noble masculine name, suggesting a defender who stands guard during the darkest hours.

**Elxenya** *(El + xen + ya)*
*Meaning:* "Daughter of the Silver Shadow."
*Lore:* A beautiful, shifting feminine name. It implies someone who operates in the twilight—neither fully in the light nor entirely in the dark.

**Elyra** *(Ely + ra)*
*Meaning:* "The Silver Voice" or "Song of the Twilight."
*Lore:* The name carries a sense of shifting light and quiet beauty. It fits her perfectly—she is the fifth-born, caught between the older boys and the baby, finding her own clear voice.

**Elysehn** *(Ely + sehn)*
*Meaning:* "The Silver Truth" or "Iron Twilight."
*Lore:* A sleek, mysterious gender-neutral name. It suggests someone who sees clearly in the dark or speaks hard truths gently.

**Fayaera** *(Faya + aera)*
*Meaning:* "The Wind’s Song" or "Grace of the Gale."
*Lore:* A highly poetic, almost musical name. It implies someone light on their feet, untamed, and hard to catch.

**Fayanor** *(Faya + nor)*
*Meaning:* "Shield of the Gale."
*Lore:* A swift, martial masculine name. Given to a warrior whose defense is as fast and unpredictable as the wind.

**Faysehnmyean** *(Fay + sehn + myean)*
*Meaning:* "True Dreamer of the Wind" or "The Iron Prince of the Gale."
*Lore:* A complex masculine name for someone whose mind is wild and untamed, yet possessing an unbreakable will.

**Heines** *(Hein + es)*
*Meaning:* "Keeper of the Memory" or "Warden of the Past."
*Lore:* A solemn name. In old Aurouvah, this might have been a title given to the elders who kept the oral histories, before becoming a given name.

**Heinsehnys** *(Hein + sehn + ys)*
*Meaning:* "The Iron Warden of Memory" or "True Edge of the Past."
*Lore:* A solemn, heavy name. Often given to the keeper of ancient histories or the punisher of broken oaths.

**Heinyra** *(Hein + yra)*
*Meaning:* "Grace of Memory" or "Song of Truth."
*Lore:* A solemn, beautiful feminine name. It suggests someone who remembers the old ways and speaks with quiet authority.

**Johirah** *(Joh + irah)*
*Meaning:* "Voice of Winter" or "Spirit's Song."
*Lore:* A sharp, clear feminine name. Often given to girls born in the deep cold, implying resilience and striking clarity.

**Lohaera** *(Loh + aera)*
*Meaning:* "The Golden Voice" or "Song of the Dawn."
*Lore:* A bright, radiant feminine name. It implies someone who brings hope, warmth, or clarity after a dark time.

**Lohaerah** *(Loh + aerah)*
*Meaning:* "The Golden Grace" or "Dawn's Light."
*Lore:* A slightly heavier, older spelling of Lohaera. Where Lohaera implies a soft "Song," Lohaerah carries the weight of ancient "Light"—radiant, commanding, and warm.

**Lohaerys** *(Loh + aerys)*
*Meaning:* "Born of the Dawn" or "Keeper of the Gold."
*Lore:* A proud, shining masculine name. Often given to a firstborn son born at sunrise, or a prince meant to bring a new golden age.

**Lohsehnes** *(Loh + sehn + es)*
*Meaning:* "True Keeper of the Gold" or "The Iron Warden of the Dawn."
*Lore:* A bright but grounded masculine name. It implies someone who fiercely guards the prosperity and hope of the realm.

**Qaehnaya** *(Qaen + naya)*
*Meaning:* "Daughter of the Storm."
*Lore:* A volatile, powerful feminine name. Given to girls born during massive tempests, implying they carry the storm's fury.

**Qaenys** *(Qaen + ys)*
*Meaning:* "Born of the Storm" or "The Lightning's Edge."
*Lore:* A sharp, dangerous, volatile name. You give this name to a child you expect to be a warrior, someone who strikes fast and leaves ruin behind them.

**Raenella** *(Rae + nella)*
*Meaning:* "Little Star" or "Beloved of the Fire."
*Lore:* A sweet, affectionate name. It might have started as a childhood nickname for a girl named Rhaenaya or Raesehnya that simply stuck and became its own formal name.

**Raesehnien** *(Rae + sehn + ien)*
*Meaning:* "True Wanderer of the Hearth" or "The Iron Breath of the Fire."
*Lore:* A gender-neutral or feminine name. It suggests someone who carries the warmth and danger of fire wherever they travel.

**Raesehnya** *(Rae + sehn + ya)*
*Meaning:* "The Iron Truth of the Flame" or "The Unbroken Star."
*Lore:* A heavy, ancient, commanding name. This is the name of a queen or a conqueror, someone who does not bend.

**Rhaeheara** *(Rhae + heara)*
*Meaning:* "Queen of Fire" or "Crown of the Hearth."
*Lore:* A highly regal, commanding feminine name. Given to a queen expected to rule with passion and undeniable force.

**Rhaemyean** *(Rhae + myean)*
*Meaning:* "Prince of Fire" or "Dreamer of the Hearth."
*Lore:* A proud masculine name. Implies someone whose ambitions burn bright and who commands the warmth of the room.

**Rhaenaya** *(Rhae + naya)*
*Meaning:* "Daughter of the Star" or "Bringer of the Hearth-Fire." 
*Lore:* A very traditional, warm Aurouvian name. It implies someone who brings light to a dark house or warmth to a cold season.

**Rhaesehnys** *(Rhae + sehn + ys)*
*Meaning:* "True Edge of the Fire" or "Iron Warden of the Hearth."

**Rhaexenys** *(Rhae + xen + ys)*

**Saeraphyn** *(Sae + ra + phyn)*
*Meaning:* "Celestial Soul of Grace" or "Holy Wing of Song."
*Lore:* An ancient, almost mythic feminine name. The mother tongue of the realm flows from the queen who bore it, carrying an aura of untouched, burning divinity.

**Vicxarion** *(Vic + xar + ion)*
*Meaning:* "Shield of the Slaughter" or "Son of the Bleeding Conquest."
*Lore:* A terrifying, iron-clad masculine name. It belongs to a warlord or conqueror who leaves nothing behind but ash and blood.

**Xaenmyean** *(Xaen + myean)*
*Meaning:* "Prince of the Eclipse" or "Dreamer of the Abyss."
*Lore:* A terrifyingly majestic masculine name. Given to a ruler born during an eclipse, or one who brings total darkness to his enemies.

**Xaenyrah** *(Xaen + yrah)*

**Xyraxes** *(Xyra + xes)*

**Xyraxys** *(Xyra + xys)*
*Meaning:* "Edge of the Scythe" or "Born of Execution."
*Lore:* A vicious, volatile masculine or gender-neutral name. Given to a ruthless enforcer or someone expected to sever the family's enemies from the world.
