# The Aurouvian Language and Names

Aurouvian is the mother tongue of Queen Seraphine, brought to Foldhall when she married Aldren III. The royal family still speaks it as a private language for intimacy, whispered asides, and muttered profanity, though two hundred years apart has caused it to drift from the original tongue. 

Linguistically, Aurouvian is tied heavily to the elements—fire, sky, deep water, stone, and memory. It has a melodic, ancient, and occasionally sharp flow.

## 1. The Roots
* **Rhae / Rae:** Fire, hearth, or a burning star.
* **Dae:** Deep water, the tide, or the moon (which pulls the tide).
* **Ae / Aen:** The sky, the heavens, or open air.
* **Fay / Faya:** Wind, breath, or the gale.
* **El / Ely:** Silver, twilight, or the shifting of light.
* **Cae:** Stone, foundation, or bedrock.
* **Qaen:** Storm, lightning, or sudden violence..
* **Hein:** Memory, truth, or the past.

## 2. The Suffixes
* **-ya / -naya:** "Daughter of" or "Bringer of" (feminine).
* **-ra / -aera:** "Song," "Voice," or "Grace."
* **-on / -an / -or:** "Son of," "Watcher," or "Shield" (masculine).
* **-ella / -nella:** "Little" or "Beloved."
* **-sehn:** "Iron" or "Truth" (often used as a mid-root).
* **-heara:** "Crown" or "Queen."
* **-es:** "Warden" or "Keeper."

## 3. Name Etymologies

**Rhaenaya** *(Rhae + naya)*
*Meaning:* "Daughter of the Star" or "Bringer of the Hearth-Fire." 
*Lore:* A very traditional, warm Aurouvian name. It implies someone who brings light to a dark house or warmth to a cold season.

**Daenaya** *(Dae + naya)*
*Meaning:* "Daughter of the Deep Tide" or "Bringer of the Moon."
*Lore:* A quieter, heavier name than Rhaenaya. It was often given to girls born during sea-storms or high tides.

**Fayaera** *(Faya + aera)*
*Meaning:* "The Wind’s Song" or "Grace of the Gale."
*Lore:* A highly poetic, almost musical name. It implies someone light on their feet, untamed, and hard to catch. 

**Aeion** *(Ae + ion)*
*Meaning:* "Son of the Sky" or "The First Light."
*Lore:* A high, noble masculine name. In old Aurouvian myth, it might have been the name of a legendary figure who first charted the stars. 

**Aeian** *(Ae + ian)*
*Meaning:* "Watcher of the Heavens."
*Lore:* A softer variation of Aeion. Where Aeion is the light itself, Aeian is the scholar or priest who observes it. 

**Aeien** *(Ae + ien)*
*Meaning:* "The Sky's Breath," "Wanderer of the Heavens," or "Falling Star."
*Lore:* The third variation of the Ae- masculine root. Its exact meaning shifts depending on context—it can mean a traveler who doesn't stay in one place, or a sharper, more martial "arrow from the sky."

**Elyra** *(Ely + ra)*
*Meaning:* "The Silver Voice" or "Song of the Twilight."
*Lore:* The name carries a sense of shifting light and quiet beauty. It fits her perfectly—she is the fifth-born, caught between the older boys and the baby, finding her own clear voice.

**Raesehnya** *(Rae + sehn + ya)*
*Meaning:* "The Iron Truth of the Flame" or "The Unbroken Star."
*Lore:* A heavy, ancient, commanding name. This is the name of a queen or a conqueror, someone who does not bend.

**Raenella** *(Rae + nella)*
*Meaning:* "Little Star" or "Beloved of the Fire."
*Lore:* A sweet, affectionate name. It might have started as a childhood nickname for a girl named Rhaenaya or Raesehnya that simply stuck and became its own formal name.

**Caenor** *(Cae + nor)*
*Meaning:* "The Stone Shield" or "Foundation."
*Lore:* A rugged, blunt masculine name. It implies someone who is unmovable, dependable, and deeply grounded. 

**Heines** *(Hein + es)*
*Meaning:* "Keeper of the Memory" or "Warden of the Past."
*Lore:* A solemn name. In old Aurouvah, this might have been a title given to the elders who kept the oral histories, before becoming a given name.

**Daeheara** *(Dae + heara)*
*Meaning:* "Queen of the Deep Water" or "Crown of the Tide."
*Lore:* A massive, imposing name. It suggests immense, quiet power—the kind of power that pulls ships under without making a sound. 

**Qaenys** *(Qaen + ys)*
*Meaning:* "Born of the Storm" or "The Lightning's Edge."
*Lore:* A sharp, dangerous, volatile name. You give this name to a child you expect to be a warrior, someone who strikes fast and leaves ruin behind them.
