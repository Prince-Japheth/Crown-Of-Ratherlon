## Dictionary of the Realm (A–Z)

**Author-only reference:** this section retains private lore and spoilers. Use `dictionary.md` for the reader-facing glossary and pronunciation key.

**Aeian.** *Aurouvian name.* **Pronunciation:** AY-ee-an. **Meaning:** “Reader of the Heavens.”

**Aeion.** *Aurouvian name.* **Pronunciation:** AY-ee-on. **Meaning:** “Shield of the Heavens” or “Son of the Sky.”

**Aekhrōn.** *Aurouvian command.* **Pronunciation:** AY-khrone. **Meaning:** Take off; climb.

**Aldren.** Regnal name taken by every crowned Brennan king.

**Aldren the Adventurous.** Aldren III (birth name Leofric). Consummated with Elen of Varen, fathered Aldric at Foldhall, then fled across the warm sea to Aurouvah. Received the Cinder (cosmetic), married Saeraphyn, commissioned the blood ritual that killed Aldric and transferred the heir-scent. Returned with Saeraphyn and their son. Romantic figure in public memory; curse-bearer in deep lore.

**Aldric Brennan.** Leofric's firstborn son by Elen of Varen, born at Foldhall. Killed by the blood ritual before his fourth nameday — his Nyxen scent was extracted and transferred to Saeraphyn's son. The court recorded "childhood fever." The Varens remember.

**Appanage.** Land/rents granted by king to younger kin.

**Aurouvah.** Wealthy kingdom on the far continent across the warm sea. A culture of ancient magic ranging from permanent cosmetic arts to deeper rituals that reach into blood. Where Leofric received the Cinder, married Saeraphyn, and commissioned the blood ritual. The Aurouvian belief of rebirth (a child who dies at birth passes its soul to the next) is deeply held and never disproven.

**Aurouvian.** **Pronunciation:** aw-ROO-vee-an. The language of Aurouvah, used in the Brennan and Greywater households for private speech and short Nyxen commands; not for public ceremony or statecraft.

**Baelzōs.** *Aurouvian command.* **Pronunciation:** BAYL-zose. **Meaning:** Strike with claw or bite.

**Banner.** The cloth device of a house. To "raise a banner" = declare for a cause.

**Bannerman.** Lesser lord sworn to a greater one.

**Behella.** *Aurouvian expression.* **Pronunciation:** beh-EL-lah. **Meaning:** An affectionate expression meaning “beloved,” used for praise or calming; not an order.

**Birth name.** Name before crowning (Rodrik for Aldren IX).

**Blood ritual.** The Aurouvian sorcery Leofric commissioned to transfer the Nyxen heir-scent from Aldric (his firstborn by Elen) to Saeraphyn's son. The extraction killed Aldric. The sorcerers warned that forcing foreign magic onto a natural system they did not understand would break something that could not be unbroken. They were right. NOT the same act as the Cinder — the Cinder was harmless cosmetic; the blood ritual was the curse.

**The Brennan Cinder.** The radiant ash-charcoal hair carried by the Brennan royal line since Aldren III married Saeraphyn of Aurouvah. Not black, not grey — a deep, luminous dark with a forged iron sheen, as if each strand held a cold fire. Chosen by Leofric to match his Nyxen's rare ash-charcoal coat; made permanent by Aurouvian sorcerers as a harmless cosmetic rider's tribute. Combined with Saeraphyn's blood to become hereditary. Strongest in firstborn heirs; fades in younger siblings and distant branches. A point of dynastic pride in-world. The Cinder is NOT the curse — it is a separate magical act with a separate moral weight.

**Bront.** Massive single-horned beast endemic to the Eastverge. Elasmotherium-inspired: stands half again as tall as a horse at the shoulder, dense russet-brown hide, thick seasonal coat, single forward-sweeping horn of bone-dense keratin longer than a man's arm. Broad flat skull, small eyes, enormously powerful neck and shoulders. Kelward broke them to the saddle generations ago and ride them instead of horses for border warfare. Named for the sound of their charge — a deep rolling thunder across the scrub. Stubborn, ill-tempered; a kick can kill a horse. But a broken bront is tireless, fearless, and carries an armored rider through terrain that would snap a horse's legs. Wild herds graze beyond the watch-stones. NOT a horse replacement kingdom-wide — bronts are a Kelward animal. A bront in the Vine Shore would be like an elephant in Venice.

**Caevārn.** *Aurouvian command.* **Pronunciation:** KAY-varn. **Meaning:** Hold; stay.

**Crownfold.** Countryside around Foldhall.

**Daeznār.** *Aurouvian command.* **Pronunciation:** DAYZ-nar. **Meaning:** Descend; land.

**Demesne.** Land held directly by a lord.

**Elyra.** *Aurouvian name.* **Pronunciation:** el-EER-ah. **Meaning:** “The Silver Voice” or “Song of the Twilight.”

**Fayrūth.** *Aurouvian command.* **Pronunciation:** FAY-rooth. **Meaning:** Fly hard; break away.

**Frugivore.** Animal that lives chiefly on fruit. Nyxens are frugivores and herbivores.

**Good-brother/sister/father/mother.** In-law terms, not praise.

**Great house.** Paramount house ruling a wide region under the king.

**Holdfast.** Small fortified dwelling; broader than "castle."

**Ledger.** Book of accounts. Prince Rowan lives in them.

**Litter.** A group of Nyxen young born together.

**Maester.** Learned men of Ratherlon — an ancient institution predating the Brennan dynasty. Healers, record-keepers, letter-writers, counselors. Every great house and many lesser lords keep a Maester. The Maester of Foldhall holds unique responsibilities: crafting call-pipes, maintaining the rider roll, and tending the pipe collection in the armoury.

**Men of Timber and Caliber.** Deepwood timber crews’ proud working name, denoting honest measure, sound workmanship and dependable people.

**Nyxen.** Great flying beast; docile, frugivore, keen ears/nose, recognizes rare scent in farmer's bloodline.

**Nyxen oil.** Grooming substance for Nyxen fur. Smell clings to Foldhall's stones.

**Oath-yard.** Open yard beside Foldhall’s Sanctuary of Vael. People cut across on errands.

**Paramount.** Highest under the king in a region.

**Pipe / call-pipe.** Mouth instrument for a Nyxen rider. Custom begins under Aldren III (the Adventurous), who brought the concept from Aurouvah. Rider's ordinal cut as protruding notches inside; those notches shape the tone. Beasts know their own across miles. Some notch-counts praised as the sweetest pipes of the blood. Crafted only by the Maester of Foldhall. Do not use "whistle." See ## THE CALL-PIPE for full lore.

**Progress.** Royal journey through the realm.

**Pup.** Young Nyxen, about Golden Retriever puppy size (bigger counting wings). Must learn to lighten before true flight.

**Qaenkhōr.** *Aurouvian command.* **Pronunciation:** KWAYN-khor. **Meaning:** The command for a storm roar: a powerful blast that throws men and objects aside.

**Realm.** Everyday speech for the Kingdom of Ratherlon.

**Reeve.** Village-level official who manages day-to-day running of a manor's farmland on behalf of the lord. Tracks planting, debts, small disputes. Not noble — a trusted commoner with authority over local order.

**Regnal name.** Name used while reigning. Always Aldren for Brennan kings.

**Rhaenaya.** *Aurouvian name.* **Pronunciation:** ray-NAY-ah. **Meaning:** “Daughter of the Star” or “Bringer of the Hearth-Fire.”

**Rider.** One who has raised/been accepted by a Nyxen. Each match written on the rider roll and given a call-pipe. Riders before Aldren III are "riders of the old way" — uncounted, un-piped.

**Rider roll.** Running count of Brennan Nyxen riders since Aldren III. Kept by the Maester of Foldhall. Physical document — vellum, the Maester's hand — stored with the pipe collection in the Foldhall armoury.

**Roar.** The Nyxen's directed sonic blast. Not flame. Three levels: normal, concussive (blows things away like a storm), and kill-roar (ruptures internal organs/heads, can explode humans at point-blank). Two beasts trading full roars can cripple each other. Normal roars are free; the storm roar and the kill roar need a spoken command.

**Roost.** Open cliff-barns or pens where Nyxens willingly go for rest, solitude, or the night. Nyxens are NOT kept or trapped; they roam freely across Foldhall's gardens and skies.

**Roost-cold.** Chill left on a bed when someone rose early for the roosts. Not the deeper cold of a spouse at war. Household slang.

**Saeraphyn.** *Aurouvian name.* **Pronunciation:** SAY-rah-fin. **Meaning:** “Celestial Soul of Grace” or “Holy Wing of Song.”

**Shout upwind.** Call so wind carries voice toward someone. Half-joke for "go find the king at the roosts."

**Shutters.** Wooden panels over windows.

**Smallfolk.** The common people of Ratherlon: farmers, tradesmen, fishermen, innkeepers — anyone without noble blood or title. Also called commonfolk. In prose, use "commoner" for a single person. Do NOT use "freeman" (echoes the wildlings' "free folk" from ASOIAF).

**Solar.** Private upper chamber; queen's work: petitions, letters, ribbons, quills.

**Steward.** Officer who runs household stores, staff, daily order.

**Wall-walk.** Path along top of castle wall.

**Xyrgōs.** *Aurouvian command.* **Pronunciation:** ZEER-gose. **Meaning:** The command for a kill roar: a deadly blast that tears the body within.

**Yard.** Open court inside castle walls.

## HANDLER LORE
- Handlers carry a wooden clapper for feeding. The pipes belong to the riders.
- A handler's code: what is said to a Nyxen in the dark is said to a Nyxen, and the roost does not hear it.
- Handlers sleep in lofts built across the rafters above the bays, a hatch in the floor above each bay, one night in three.
- Fruit costs are now watched by the counting-house. Hart alone eats a bushel to a bushel and a half a day.
- Handlers cannot ride and cannot bond. Old Oren's rule for young handlers is "love them with your hands open."
