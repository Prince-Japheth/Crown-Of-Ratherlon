# Aldren VIII's siblings, the age table, and the offices

Year 0 (Y0) is the day Rodrik is crowned. Book 1 opens in the first year after it. Names marked (placeholder) are mine.

## 1. You were right about Aldren VIII's siblings

`houses-and-genealogy.md` has Aldren VIII as the eldest child of Aldren VII and Freya Norvalen, with two younger siblings, **Alys** and **Robert**. Both are non-riders. Their Nyxen scent was too weak to bond, so the family's strength went to the king's own brood, which is why Aldren VIII's children are so many and so nearly all riders. The roost handlers have a joke about it: *"Aldren the Eighth took all the scent in the house."* Use it once, in a handler's mouth, and never twice.

## 2. Alys and Robert are alive at Y0

Both are younger than Aldren VIII, who was 54 when he died. So at Y0:

| Person | Age at Y0 | Status |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Alys Brennan**, Dowager Duchess of Eastgirt | 50 | Alive, in Eastgirt |
| **Prince Robert Brennan**, married to Lysa Dorsen | 47 | Alive, at the Dorsen seat in the Rivermark |

They can still be alive when the Twin War starts. At Y21 they would be 71 and 68, which is old but ordinary. **Neither is planned to die by illness.** See section 7.

### Alys

- Married at **seventeen** to **Jonos Kelward**, then **Duke of Eastgirt**, who was **forty, twenty-three years her senior**. Aldren VII arranged it to bind the Eastverge after a hard run of raid years. Alys had no say in it.
- She bore Simon at 22 and Alton at 26. Jonos was old when they were born, which is why both boys grew up with an elderly, soldierly father and a young, sharp mother.
- **Jonos died ten years ago in the First Ashen War**, aged 63, leading the Kelward bront charge into the trap the Ashen had laid for Oak. A bolt from one of the hidden iron scorpion-ballistas (see `the-first-ashen-war-cause.md`) was meant for Oak and struck him instead. He is the only duke of the Eastverge to die in a war in living memory.
- **Simon was eighteen** and became Duke. Alys ran Eastgirt beside him for years and still does, in the background. She is a tall, spare woman with her father's brown eyes and none of his Cinder. She has no Nyxen and no illusions.
- She would be told of Simon's wound after the Redcut and, much later, of his death. Her reaction is one of the book's emotional pillars (see `simon-kelward-death.md`).

### Robert

- Married **Lysa Dorsen** at 24. Lysa's father, **old Lord Alden Dorsen**, was the **Hand of the King** under Aldren VIII. When Alden died, Aldren VIII named his son Rodrik Hand. That is the line in `world-building-4.md`, now with a family connection: Robert's wife is the dead Hand's daughter, and her brother **Maren Dorsen** is the current Lord of the Rivermark.
- Robert styles himself Prince Robert of Dorsen's Reach (placeholder). He is easygoing, fond of fish, dice and company, a better host than a politician, and he knows it.
- Four children, all Brennan-blooded cousins of Rodrik's generation, none riders: two sons and two daughters. Names are open.
- His role: the Rivermark's honest broker. Both twins will send envoys to him and to Maren. He should be liked by everyone and trusted by few.

## 3. Why Simon is Duke

Because Jonos fell in the First Ashen War when Simon was eighteen, and the Duchy passes to the firstborn son. Alys held the household. The Kelward lords accepted a boy duke because he had ridden beside his father in that same war and had come out alive.

## 5. The Master of Coin

- The previous Master of Coin was **Lord Warin Lansor** (placeholder). He held the office for thirty years under Aldren VII and VIII.
- **Aldren VIII chose Rowan as Lansor's pupil.** Rowan had no Nyxen and no future in the sky, and the king wanted the second son to have a useful job. Rowan trained under Lansor for **seven years, from fifteen**.
- Lansor died **a year before Rodrik's coronation** in an accident: his coach went off a washed-out ford on the road back from Rosegate with the Vine Shore tithe rolls aboard. Not an illness.
- Aldren VIII did not name a successor. He liked keeping the seal and the ledgers close, and he told Rowan, "Keep the books." Then he died.
- Rowan has kept them for the year since. **He holds the work and no title.** He took the stool at the foot of the council table because no one told him to, and no one has told him to rise. Lansor left Rowan his ledger keys in his will, which the Small Council has never forgotten.
- This is what lets Rowan say, with total accuracy, that he has no wish to be a stone that starts a landslide: he knows the work better than anyone alive, and every man at the table knows it, too.

## 7. Death variety (new standing rule)

The canon already has several deaths by sickness (Aldren VIII's "violent stomach illness," Seren's winter fever). **Do not give any further named character a death by illness unless the story needs it.** Use accidents (Lansor), battle (Jonos, Aldren I, Aldren VI), murder, drowning, falls, exposure, poison, childbirth (Rowan's wife, already canon), or being killed by a person.
