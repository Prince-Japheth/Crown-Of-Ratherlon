# CULTURAL LIFE & THE SPRAWLING CAST

Ratherlon is built to have the exact day-to-day texture, cultural richness, and sprawling cast of an ASOIAF/HOTD book. While it is an original fantasy world, it is designed to feel like a high medieval society bursting with life, politics, and history.

## 1. The Sprawling Cast
We are not just locked in on Rodrik and his siblings. The world must feel incredibly populated. 
- **Secondary and Tertiary Characters:** We need a massive cast of named characters across all walks of life—great lords, ambitious second sons, squires, maesters, kingsguards, watchmen, master-at-arms, court gossips, and commoners. 
- **Interactions:** The royal family must constantly interact with the wider world, navigating alliances, rivalries, and minor slights. Every character should feel like they have their own agenda.
- **Inspiration:** Look at the sheer volume of characters in HOTD (the Hulls, the Strongs, the Hightowers, the Blackwoods, the Brackens, etc.). We must match this density of named, distinct houses and individuals in Ratherlon. 

## 2. Day-to-Day Medieval Life
The world is not just war and quiet rooms. It is a living, breathing feudal society. Include and center events around:
- **Tourneys & Melees:** Used for settling grudges, showing off martial prowess, and political maneuvering.
- **Duels & Trial by Combat:** The brutal reality of justice and honor.
- **Namedays & Feasts:** Massive social gatherings where the real politicking happens over wine and roasted meat.
- **Ceremonies & Court Mechanics:** Petitions, royal hunts, knightings, and the grinding machinery of feudal governance. 

When writing chapters, weave these elements into the background and foreground to ensure the world never feels empty or strictly focused on just the main protagonists. The setting *is* a character.
