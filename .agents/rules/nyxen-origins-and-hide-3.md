## HOW TO APPLY

This is Book 4 material (the Farmer era, Year 0). The giant vegetation, hide properties, and escalation from crop defense to greed-driven extinction are deep background. In Book 1, characters already know Nyxen hide is extraordinary — they live in a world where no giant vegetation remains and the Nyxens are domesticated. The reader pieces together the deep history gradually.
