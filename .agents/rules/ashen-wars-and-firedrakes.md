# Ashen wars and the firedrakes (decision file)

Everything below is a proposal for you to approve. I chose it so that it fits the chapters already written (1 to 15) and every line of canon in the `.agents` files, and so that readers meet the firedrakes in real time, with no time skip.

## 1. Naming: Option A

- **The First Ashen War** is the old war, about ten years before the book opens, under Aldren VIII with Oak in the sky. Rodrik fought in it as a young prince against his father's orders. Ser Corren lost half his ear in it. Chapters 1 to 15 already treat it this way.
- **The Second Ashen War** is the long firedrake war that Harwin fights in as a young man at Rodrik's side, and after which Edric carries Foldhall's truce terms to the Eastverge. It replaces the phrase "First Ashen War" everywhere the outline uses it for the twins' youth.

The brief "Eastverge troubles" now being written (raids, the Redcut) are not a war with a number. They are the Ashen testing a realm whose Oak has gone.

## 2. Answering "can the two things happen at once?"

Yes, and they work better as a chain. Oak's absence and the firedrakes are two different causes at two different times:

| Phase | When | What it is | What the Ashen king is doing |
|-------|------|------------|------------------------------|
| 1. The gap | Aldren VIII dies, Oak flies away. The book's opening year | Border-bands, deniable raids, bought silver, forged cloth, the Redcut | Hiding behind deniability. He is afraid of what Foldhall might still send. |
| 2. The Opening | About four years into Rodrik's reign. The twins are three or four | The volcano event. Firedrakes appear in the Ashen mountains | Stops denying. He now has an answer to the Nyxens. |
| 3. The fire years | The twins' childhood and youth | Raids with firedrakes, growing numbers, a realm learning to bleed | Openly at war, patient, building a herd |
| 4. The Second Ashen War | The twins are young men | The great campaign. Harwin at Rodrik's side, Edric carrying the truce afterward | Almost wins, for the first time in Ashen history |
| 5. Twin War opportunism | After the truce, when the realm splits | The Ashen watch the brothers and wait | Ready to strike the broken realm |

This also settles the open question in `book-outline-2.md` ("Firedrake appearance timing: Twin War opportunism vs later book"). The answer is both: first appearance in phase 2, large-scale use in phase 4, opportunism in phase 5.

## 3. The Opening: what happens and what is known

**Author-only truth.** A gate opens beneath one of the Ashen volcano sites and creatures from a far older age walk out of it. Nobody in the world has a word for gates or for those ages, and none should ever be given one. Do not use "portal," "dinosaur," or any word that sounds as if it came from outside the world.

**What the Ashen believe.** The fire gods of the mountain heard the Ashen and sent beasts to match the great beasts of Ratherlon. They call the first herd *the Given*.

**What the realm calls them.** *Firedrakes* and *great lizards*, from the tales of the first soldiers to survive them.

**Three kinds.** Describe them, never name the real animals.
- The great two-legged hunters with enormous jaws and tiny forearms, taller than a bront, ridden by chosen warriors who sit in a sling harness behind the skull.
- Fast pack-runners the size of a big hound or a man, claws like sickles, that hunt in dozens and are not ridden.
- Leather-winged gliders with long beaks, which drop fire from above onto roofs and camps.

**How they breathe fire.** They spew a burning fluid that sticks. Do not call it breath of the gods. It is a thing the beast makes.

**Why the Nyxen is the answer, and why it still costs.** Canon already says the Nyxen's fur resists fire and its roar can blow a flame-stream back. The Nyxen is not safe. The rider is. A rider burns where the fur does not.

## 4. First contact is a shown event, not a known fact

Do not have the realm already know the firedrakes from old battles. The first time Ratherlon sees one, it should be the first time the reader does.

- Place: a Kelward watch-stone on the dry road. A good name is **Marrow Watch**, a place chapter in the outline's own words ("Kelward watchtower").
- Lens: an ordinary soldier, not a royal. A sergeant, or a boy on his first watch.
- Order of events: the east wind carries ash. Birds leave the scrub at dusk. The horses scream before the men understand why. Then a light crosses the sky, lower than any star.
- Aftermath: one or two survivors reach Eastgirt. Their account is garbled and disbelieved. Veterans of the First Ashen War say it is a trick of the Ashen to frighten them. Only when the second watch is burned do the lords listen.
- No hints before this. The sulphur pots in chapter 11 are enough: the Ashen already use volcanic sulphur, and nothing else needs seeding.

## 5. Who may not die from it

Do not use the Opening to kill a family member before the twins are grown. The Opening is a shock to the realm. The deaths that follow it are for non-royals unless you choose otherwise.

## 7. Chapter 14 optional note

Chapter 14 has Orston saying the shepherds above the Redcut "speak of a great shadow." It is an Oak hint.
