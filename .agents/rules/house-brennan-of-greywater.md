# House Brennan of Greywater (cadet branch)

Tomas and Brenna Brennan are locked names. See `brennan-hair-greywater-and-travel.md` for the controlling canon.

## Origin

- Founded by **Garlan Brennan**, the younger brother of **Aldren IV**. `houses-and-genealogy.md` already has him: Garlan, third child of Aldren III and Saeraphyn, married to **Mina Selborn**.
- Aldren IV was the Grounded King (see `brennan-kings-and-internal-strife.md`). Garlan flew his own Nyxen in his brother’s place and was the realm's real sword for thirty years.
- When the Three Dukes' Quarrel ended, Garlan refused the regency that some lords urged on him. He said he would not take a crown his brother was still alive to wear. Aldren IV rewarded him with a tower and mill-lands on the upper Fold, a day's ride north of Foldhall: **Greywater**. Garlan's sons kept the surname **Brennan** by royal licence, which is why the branch has never become Selborn. (The tree shows Garlan's marriage but not this.)

## What they kept and what they lost

- **Aurouvian.** Saeraphyn lived into old age and raised Garlan's children as her favorites. The Greywater Brennans have spoken Aurouvian at home ever since, and they alone outside the royal family still do.
- **The drift.** Two hundred years apart, their Aurouvian and the royal family's have drifted. The Greywaters speak the older, gentler form. Royal brothers who swear in Aurouvian at one another in public have sometimes been answered, politely and in the same tongue, by a cousin standing six feet away.
- **The Cinder.** Gone. A Greywater Brennan's hair is a plain dark brown that, in strong sun, shows a faint warm umber. It is a ghost of the family color, and people notice it only if they are looking for it.
- **The scent.** Gone for five generations. No Greywater has bonded with a Nyxen in five generations. Brenna is also a non-rider.

## Their place in the realm

- **Hereditary Wardens of the Roosts.** The post is old, small and mostly ceremonial. The Warden is the family's formal overseer of the handlers and keeps the roost rolls. Handlers like Oren and Wynn work under him on paper, and in practice the Warden approves feed costs, attends the first flights of pups and complains about the cold. He is the Brennan who loves Nyxens most and cannot ride one.
- The present Warden is **Lord Tomas Brennan of Greywater**, about 55, a cheerful, vague, round man with a scholar's stoop and a drawer full of Nyxen anecdotes. He is much beloved by the handlers and mildly embarrassing to the family.
- His heir is his niece, **Brenna Brennan**, about the twins' age, a quick-tempered girl who has been hanging around the roosts since she could walk. She is a non-rider and continues the family’s work with the roosts.

## Why they matter

1. **Comedy and warmth.** They are the cousins the family forgets to be formal with.
2. **Aurouvian.** They can overhear and understand private royal speech. Brothers who swear in Aurouvian and siblings who snort at it have never had to guard against anyone outside the walls. The Greywaters are the exception, and that has consequences when the family splits.
3. **A succession footnote.** The Greywaters are the nearest male-line Brennans. They have never wanted the crown and would make poor kings. At the end of the Twin War, a realm without a clear heir might mention them. Both twins die on the cliff; the returning body carries neither identity and is crowned Aldren X.
4. **Brenna is a non-rider (locked).** Her distant cadet ancestry does not qualify her for a bond. Her role is in roost stewardship and handler work; the branch supports Aeion.

## Twin War allegiance and first appearance

First on page in chapter 19: Lord Tomas Brennan, Warden of the Roosts. Greywater supports **Aeion**, who holds Foldhall and listens to Tomas and the handlers; Aeian treats them as tools. Tomas uses their Aurouvian to overhear Aeian's camp once and regrets it. The branch fights little and has no riders. Handlers follow Tomas, and their control of feeding gives the Warden practical power.

## Chapter 19 appearance

Tomas rides a day south from Greywater to Foldhall and settles a roost repair dispute by matching paid timber, delivery records and permission to use it. His sister and infant niece Brenna have arrived separately by a two-day litter journey. Brenna is about the twins’ age and remains a non-rider throughout the story. Tomas is affectionate and funny, but competent at the work he has held for twenty-eight years. “Vague” describes his social manner, never inability to do his job.
